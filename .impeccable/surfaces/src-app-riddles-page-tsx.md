# /riddles surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Visitors arrive from ads, WhatsApp and social, mostly on phones, and decide in one visit whether to pre-order.
Job: pre-order "חידה! לכל המשפחה" vol. 1 at the pre-sale price. Proof: the cover and real interior pages. Constraints: price, ship month, single-shipment rule and coupon exclusion stated plainly; no invented reviews.
Client-approved structure: mockup A (`.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.html`). Client feedback: pre-order and November arrival must be impossible to miss; orders with in-stock books ship together in one November parcel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a pre-order is a ticket, not a product card. The page sells a seat on the first print run and says when it arrives, refusing the stock product-page layout of image left, specs right.
OWN-WORLD: the book's cover split, orange #DE8F47 over navy #31508F, inside KidCode's #545454 thick outlines and hard offset shadows; red #B83C3A CTA, sun-yellow ticket stub, Karantina hand-lettered titles over Rubik.
STORY: this is a new riddle book for the whole family. You can pre-order it now for ₪102, it arrives during November, and you can see the inside. Then you order.
FIRST VIEWPORT: a sun-yellow packing-tape strip (yellow, not navy: it must outshout the orange hero, per client feedback that pre-order and November be impossible to miss) repeating "רכישה מוקדמת · מגיע במהלך נובמבר 2026" runs across the top of the hero. Huge white "חידה!" title, the tilted cover with a red pre-order sticker, a price ticket whose stub reads "מגיע בנובמבר", and the red CTA under it. On mobile the cover comes first, then title, ticket and CTA; the tape stays at the very top. No sticky bar: the WhatsApp and accessibility buttons own the bottom corners.
FORM: assigned (A), first on the ordered list, seed 71249489.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
